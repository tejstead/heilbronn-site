Missing coordinates

Every entry on this site shows verified coordinates — but not always the record holder's own. This page lists what's missing. If any of it is yours (or you can reach whoever it belongs to), a submission is a plain pull request — see CONTRIBUTING; coordinates are verified in exact arithmetic and go live within minutes of merging, credited to their finder.
