# Attribution

Facts snapshot of the three Heilbronn pages of Erich Friedman's Packing
Center (squares, triangles, convex regions): per-entry values, credit
sentences and symmetry labels, parsed from the HTML. No images or prose
beyond the per-entry sentences were copied.

The pages went offline in 2026 (all three returned 404 by 2026-09-08). The
newest `parsed-*.json` here (2026-08-23) is therefore the final snapshot and
is the frozen input `build/ingest.py` reads; the daily watcher and the
fetch/parse script that produced these files were removed with it. The
parser is preserved in git history as `scripts/friedman_sync.py`.
